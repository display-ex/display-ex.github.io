Comparison FAQ

Q: Why not just use Windows settings?

A: For simple setups they’re fine. Display EX saves time by switching full display configurations per app and keeping DPI/HDR/refresh consistent.

Q: Will Display EX replace my GPU control panel?

A: No, it complements it — focusing on profiles, scaling, HDR and refresh coordination across multiple displays.

Q: Is it safe to install?

A: Yes. The installer is code‑signed and distributed via a single canonical URL. See Security for details.
